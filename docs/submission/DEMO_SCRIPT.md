# Loom demo script (~1:40, his voice)

Rules: Loom screen recording, natural cursor pace, never compress pauses.
Read live numbers off the page at record time; do not memorize values that
move (prices, basket lines). Basescan opens in the same tab, never a new one.

## Scene 1: hook on the landing (0:00 to 0:15)

On screen: https://try-weft.vercel.app landing, hero plus the live basket
specimen card. Slow scroll: hero, situation headline, stop on "Weft is the
layer that was missing."

Say:

"You can buy Apple onchain now. Real shares, in your wallet, no brokerage.
Ask the app to show you your statement, your dividends, what you actually
own, and it can't. That gap is why I built Weft."

## Scene 2: the statement (0:15 to 0:45)

On screen: click "See a live statement", the tape prints 76 real rows.

Say:

"This is a real address that's been buying these stocks since August. No
login, no signature: just paste the address and Weft prints the whole
statement. Every buy, every sell, every corporate action, dated and ordered.
And this blue proof on each line? It opens the exact transaction on
Basescan. Nothing is stored. The chain is re-read every single time."

Action: let the tape print, click one proof link, hold Basescan two seconds,
go back.

## Scene 3: the basket (0:45 to 1:05)

On screen: back on /weft, scroll to the basket table.

Say:

"Now the basket itself. Thirty-five percent Apple, thirty-five NVIDIA,
fifteen Microsoft, fifteen Tesla: the four deepest stock pools on Base. The
multiplier column is the part nobody else shows you, and it's about to
matter."

Action: slow scroll across the table, pause on the multiplier column.

## Scene 4: the proof run (1:05 to 1:30)

On screen: /weft/proof, the five-row loop.

Say:

"No dividend has ever run on the mainnet registry yet, so Weft proved the
whole loop on Base's testnet, where the same B20 precompiles are live.
One thousand dollars woven in. Dividend lands: ten twenty. Split lands:
fifteen thirty. Unwound. And notice: the raw token balances never moved.
All of it flowed through the multiplier, exactly how Coinbase designed it."

Action: scroll the proof rows slowly, pause on the dividend row, then the
split row.

## Scene 5: close (1:30 to 1:45)

On screen: back to the landing, hold the final frame with the two buttons
and the footer.

Say:

"Weft. Real stocks, one token, and the paperwork that was missing. The app
is live, the source is public, and every claim re-verifies itself against
the chain."

Action: none. Hold five seconds.
